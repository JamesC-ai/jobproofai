First-job evidence

Build a truthful evidence map when you have no formal work history

A short resume is better than a padded one. Start with the role's actual requirements, then map only work you personally completed in coursework, projects, activities, caregiving, household responsibilities, or other permitted settings.

Evidence inventory

Separate completed work from hoped-for skills

  1. Copy only de-identified requirements from one current job description.
  2. List tasks you actually completed, what you produced, which tools you used, and who could review the work.
  3. For each requirement, mark the evidence explicit, partial, or missing.
  4. Keep a requirement missing when a course title, interest, or self-rating does not prove applied work.

Truthful wording

Describe the setting you actually used

Label coursework as coursework, a personal project as a personal project, and an informal responsibility as informal. Do not convert these into employment, clients, internships, leadership, measured outcomes, or professional authority. Ask a human reviewer to confirm that every line matches the underlying work.

Open gaps

Turn missing evidence into a next question

A missing match may identify a small project, supervised practice, course assignment, volunteer option, or interview question to consider. JobProofAI does not decide which activity is suitable and never supplies fabricated evidence to close the gap.

No ATS or hiring prediction

JobProofAI does not verify identity or work, authenticate records, score ATS performance, decide role fit, contact employers, submit applications, or predict interviews and hiring. Remove names, contact details, IDs, references, private messages, and confidential records before browser-local review.
