Review method

Test one claim at a time

  1. Identify the exact action, tool, scope, and outcome stated in the bullet.
  2. Locate a real project, work sample, record, or person who can verify the claim.
  3. Label an unsupported metric or ownership claim as missing rather than estimating it.
  4. Rewrite only after a human reviewer confirms the wording matches the evidence.

Boundary

No automatic quality or hiring score

The audit does not prove competence, seniority, job fit, recruiter preference, ATS compatibility, or interview likelihood. Never invent an employer, date, metric, credential, tool, responsibility, or result.

Keep source material private

Remove names, contact details, client-confidential text, references, IDs, and background-check information before pasting. JobProofAI does not submit applications.
