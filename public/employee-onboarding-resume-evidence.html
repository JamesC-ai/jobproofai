Boundary

The map does not certify training, assess employee performance, prove causation, score ATS compatibility, or predict hiring. Never invent participants, materials, dates, completion, productivity, approvals, attribution, or outcomes.

Protect employee information

Do not paste employee names, evaluations, contact details, HR records, credentials, private messages, or confidential training materials.
