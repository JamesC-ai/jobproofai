Review method

Bound the claim before writing it

  1. Name the product, interface, standard, or user need only at an approved level of detail.
  2. State your actual review, design, testing, or implementation contribution.
  3. Record the tools, assistive technologies, versions, and checks you truly used.
  4. Label untested environments, unresolved defects, and user research gaps as open.

Boundary

No legal, technical, or user-impact certification

JobProofAI does not certify accessibility, legal compliance, usability, inclusion, or assistive-technology coverage. Never invent standards, test results, users, defects fixed, or outcomes.

Remove sensitive product details
