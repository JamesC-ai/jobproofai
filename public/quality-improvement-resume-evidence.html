Boundary

No quality, safety, or performance verification

The map does not audit quality systems, certify compliance, prove causation, assess performance, score ATS compatibility, or predict hiring. Never invent defects, baselines, rates, dates, methods, approvals, attribution, or outcomes.

Protect operational records

Do not paste customer records, inspection reports, incident details, patient data, credentials, private dashboards, or confidential process information.
