Privacy

Your text stays in this browser

The current version performs requirement extraction and evidence matching locally in your browser. Resume text, job descriptions, and generated reports are not uploaded by the tool.

Remove contact details, addresses, IDs, references, recruiter messages, confidential employer material, health information, immigration records, and background-check data before use. A copied or downloaded report is controlled by you.

The optional license check sends only an activation code and product name to the shared license service. PayPal processes checkout information under its own privacy terms. Resume text, job descriptions, evidence matches, and paid workbook content are not sent by the tool.

Ordinary hosting may process standard request metadata such as IP address, browser type, requested path, and security events. Do not use the service for unlawful monitoring or discrimination.
