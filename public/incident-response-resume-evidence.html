Review method

Describe actions without exposing the incident

  1. Use a broad, de-identified incident category and period.

Boundary

No security, safety, causation, or hiring verification

The map does not investigate incidents, certify security or safety, prove causation, assess performance, score ATS compatibility, or predict hiring. Never invent incidents, severity, actions, timelines, approvals, attribution, resolution, or outcomes.

Protect sensitive incident details

Do not paste vulnerabilities, access details, customer or patient data, legal records, credentials, internal reports, or information that could enable harm.
