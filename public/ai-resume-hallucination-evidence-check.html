Review method

Compare the rewrite with the source, claim by claim

  1. Highlight every number, named tool, title, responsibility, client, and outcome added or materially changed.
  2. Map each claim to an existing resume line or another approved record you are allowed to use.
  3. Restore narrower wording when the evidence shows contribution rather than ownership or an estimate rather than a measured result.
  4. Delete unsupported claims instead of asking another model to make them sound plausible.

Boundary

No ATS, truth, or hiring certification

JobProofAI does not verify employment, authenticate records, score ATS performance, decide fit, submit applications, or predict interviews and hiring. Never invent dates, employers, clients, metrics, tools, authority, credentials, responsibilities, or outcomes.

Keep source material private and minimal

Remove names, contact details, addresses, IDs, references, recruiter messages, confidential employer records, and unrelated personal information before browser-local review.
