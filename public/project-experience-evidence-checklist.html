Review method

Separate team output from personal evidence

  1. State the project purpose and your actual role without claiming the whole team's work.
  2. List the decisions, tasks, tools, and deliverables you personally handled.
  3. Record dates, scale, and results only when a reliable source supports them.
  4. Keep confidential details out while preserving enough context for human review.

Boundary

No automatic credit, impact, or job-fit decision

A project description does not prove ownership, leadership, skill level, ATS compatibility, or hiring likelihood. Never invent collaborators, clients, dates, metrics, tools, credentials, or business outcomes.

Use only shareable evidence

Do not paste source code, private repositories, customer records, contracts, credentials, or employer-confidential material.
