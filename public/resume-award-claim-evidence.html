Review method

Verify the recognition, not a story around it

  1. Record the official name, issuer, and date.
  2. Confirm whether the award was individual, team, internal, public, competitive, or participation-based.
  3. Use only permitted evidence for the selection basis.
  4. Map it only to requirements it directly supports.

Boundary

No award, ranking, or performance verification

The tool does not contact issuers, verify rankings, assess performance, score ATS compatibility, or predict interviews. Never invent an award, issuer, date, selection process, rank, metric, recipient scope, or outcome.

Keep identifiers private

Do not paste employee IDs, private nominations, judging notes, account credentials, internal rankings, or confidential correspondence.
