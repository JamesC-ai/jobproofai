Review method

Make scope and attribution explicit

  1. Record team size, function, reporting context, and period only when accurate.
  2. Separate your decision, facilitation, or coaching from collective execution.
  3. Attach a reliable source to each budget, growth, quality, delivery, or retention metric.
  4. Mark shared, partial, or unknown attribution honestly for a human reviewer.

Boundary

No leadership rating or employment prediction

The map does not verify management quality, assess references, predict interviews, or determine fit. Never invent direct reports, budgets, promotions, dates, metrics, employers, or business results.

Keep people and company data private

Remove employee names, performance records, compensation, private financials, customer data, and confidential strategy before pasting.
