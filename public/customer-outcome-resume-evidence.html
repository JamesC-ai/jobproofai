Boundary

No causal, revenue, or hiring prediction

The map does not prove causation, verify analytics, assess performance, score ATS compatibility, or predict hiring. Never invent customers, dates, revenue, retention, satisfaction, adoption, attribution, testimonials, or outcomes.

Protect customer data

Do not paste customer names, emails, contracts, account records, health or financial data, private metrics, credentials, or support conversations.
