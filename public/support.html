JobProofAI support

Evidence-map question

Email support@pagecheckai.com with the browser type, whether the issue affects mapping, copy, or download, and a short de-identified example. Do not send a full resume, job application, address, phone, email, employer-confidential text, recruiter messages, account credentials, IDs, references, or background-check information.

Checkout and activation

After a completed $19 Application Evidence Pack checkout, enter the JP- activation code shown on the confirmation page or sent to the PayPal email. If activation or download fails, send only the PayPal receipt ID, checkout email, product name, and a short technical description. Do not send resume or job-description text.

Product boundary

JobProofAI organizes text evidence. It does not access ATS systems, score employability, predict interviews or hiring, verify credentials, contact employers, submit applications, negotiate salary, or provide immigration, employment, legal, financial, or career-placement advice.
