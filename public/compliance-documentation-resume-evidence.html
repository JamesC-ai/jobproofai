Review method

Distinguish documentation from certification

  1. Identify the policy, procedure, register, or evidence package.
  2. Record your drafting, review, maintenance, or coordination role.
  3. Name approval status without implying regulator acceptance.
  4. Use only current, externally shareable descriptions.

Boundary

No legal, regulatory, or hiring verification

The map does not provide legal advice, certify compliance, validate audits, determine regulatory acceptance, score ATS compatibility, or predict hiring. Never invent standards, approvals, audits, findings, dates, responsibilities, attribution, or outcomes.

Protect controlled documents

Do not paste audit findings, legal advice, regulated records, credentials, customer data, internal controls, or confidential policies.
