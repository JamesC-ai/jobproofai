Review method

Separate title history from interpretation

  1. Use official titles and accurate effective dates.
  2. Record responsibilities that actually changed.
  3. Support new scope with approved records or references.
  4. Leave performance reasons or comparative claims out unless documented and permitted.

Boundary

No performance, reference, or hiring prediction

The map does not verify promotions, assess performance, read references, score ATS compatibility, or predict hiring. Never invent titles, dates, reporting lines, pay, reasons, responsibilities, awards, metrics, or outcomes.

Protect employment records

Do not paste compensation, employee IDs, performance reviews, manager messages, HR records, references, or private promotion documents.
