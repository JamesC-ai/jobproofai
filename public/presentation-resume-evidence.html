Review method

Check the claim in four parts

  1. State the real audience, setting, and purpose without guessing attendance.
  2. Identify what you personally prepared, presented, or facilitated.
  3. Separate feedback you received from decisions or outcomes you cannot verify.
  4. Use only approved, de-identified artifacts or public materials as evidence.

Boundary

No reach, persuasion, or outcome prediction

JobProofAI does not verify presentation quality, audience reaction, communication skill, adoption, revenue, or hiring fit. Never invent audience size, seniority, client names, feedback, conversion, or results.

Keep presentation material private

Remove confidential slides, customer names, unreleased plans, internal links, recordings, and attendee details before review.
