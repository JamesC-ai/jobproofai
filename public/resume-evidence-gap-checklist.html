Gap questions

Ask for proof before rewriting

  1. Did you perform the stated task in a real role, project, volunteer setting, or other verifiable context?
  2. What action did you personally take, and what source could confirm it?
  3. Is a result documented, or would a metric be an unsupported estimate?
  4. Does the posting require a credential, authorization, or tool experience you do not have?

Decision

Leave unsupported requirements missing

Reword only evidence that already exists. If support cannot be verified, keep the gap visible and decide whether to apply without that qualification.

No automatic suitability score
