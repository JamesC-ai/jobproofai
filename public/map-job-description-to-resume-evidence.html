Requirement mapping

Map each stated requirement to resume evidence

Separate what the posting asks for from what your resume actually supports. Treat missing support as a question, not permission to invent a claim.

Build the free evidence map

Review method

Use one row per requirement

  1. Extract the exact responsibility, skill, credential, tool, or outcome stated in the posting.
  2. Link only resume lines that explicitly support it with a real role, action, context, or result.
  3. Mark indirect support as partial and record what a human reviewer still needs to verify.
  4. Mark absent support as missing; do not manufacture an employer, date, metric, credential, or skill.

Final review

Keep the hiring decision outside the map

Use the map to inspect evidence quality and prepare truthful questions. It does not determine fit, recruiter preference, ATS compatibility, interview likelihood, salary, sponsorship, or hiring outcome.

Browser-local and evidence-only

Remove contact details, addresses, IDs, references, confidential hiring notes, and unrelated personal data before pasting text. JobProofAI does not upload or submit an application.
