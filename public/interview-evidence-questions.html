Question set

Verify context, action, result, and limits

  1. What was the situation, and what part of it did you personally own?
  2. What action did you take, and which tool, process, or judgment did it require?
  3. What result is documented, and what cannot be claimed from the available evidence?
  4. What would a former colleague, work sample, or record confirm or contradict?

Missing evidence

Practice an honest boundary

If you lack the requested experience, prepare an accurate explanation of adjacent evidence and what you would need to learn. Do not rehearse a fabricated example.

No interview prediction

These are self-verification prompts, not a list of questions an employer will ask. JobProofAI does not predict interviews, evaluate personality, coach deception, or promise an offer.
