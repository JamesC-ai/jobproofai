Truthful tailoring

Prioritize relevant proof without changing the facts

Tailoring should improve selection and clarity, not manufacture alignment. Start with a requirement-to-evidence map and keep every employer, date, title, action, tool, credential, and result accurate.

Map evidence for free

Edit order

Reorder, clarify, verify

  1. Move the strongest relevant evidence where a human reviewer can find it quickly.
  2. Replace vague wording with a specific action only when the underlying record supports it.
  3. Use a posting's terminology only when it accurately describes your real work.
  4. Verify every number, date, title, credential, tool, and scope before finalizing.

Avoid

Do not copy requirements into the resume as claims

Do not add keywords disconnected from experience, inflate ownership, convert team results into personal results, or imply a certification or authorization you do not hold.

Human review remains required
