Review method

Verify the exact claim

  1. Copy the official credential name and issuing organization.
  2. Confirm earned, expiration, renewal, suspended, or planned status.
  3. Keep certificate IDs private unless a trusted process requires them.
  4. Map the credential only to requirements it actually supports.

Boundary

No credential or competence verification

The tool does not contact issuers, verify certificates, test competence, score ATS compatibility, or predict interviews. Never invent a credential, issuer, date, status, ID, course, score, skill, or requirement match.

Keep identifiers private

Do not paste certificate numbers, account credentials, identity documents, transcripts, test records, or private issuer correspondence.
