Review method

Use observable work evidence

  1. Identify the remote or hybrid context and your actual responsibilities.

  3. Describe a real coordination, documentation, or delivery example.

Boundary

No productivity, trust, or fit rating

JobProofAI does not monitor work, verify tools, assess autonomy, predict performance, score ATS compatibility, or determine remote-role fit. Never invent employers, dates, tools, time zones, metrics, responsibilities, or outcomes.
