Conflict-resolution evidence

Describe conflict work without claiming an outcome you cannot prove

Map the situation, your role, the steps you personally took, and the approved record of what followed. Participation in a difficult conversation is not proof that you resolved the conflict.

Review method

Separate your actions from the group result

  1. State the work context and your formal responsibility without naming private participants.
  2. Identify the listening, clarification, escalation, documentation, or follow-up steps you actually performed.
  3. Use an approved record for any agreement, delivery, retention, or performance outcome.
  4. Label shared decisions, unresolved issues, and missing attribution instead of filling the gaps.

Boundary

No mediation, conduct, or workplace-outcome certification

JobProofAI does not judge people, verify a dispute, certify mediation, assess workplace conduct, or predict hiring. Never invent participants, complaints, authority, agreements, disciplinary actions, retention, savings, or results.

Keep sensitive workplace details private

Remove names, protected characteristics, medical details, complaints, legal advice, investigation records, private messages, and confidential employer information before review.
