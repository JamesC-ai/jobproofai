Review method

Ask four evidence questions

  1. Where did you use the skill, and what task did you personally perform?
  2. How recently did you use it, and what version or environment was involved?
  3. What artifact, result, colleague, or public work can support the claim?
  4. Which requirement remains partial or missing and needs learning rather than wording?

Boundary

No proficiency, credential, or fit prediction

JobProofAI does not verify certificates, test skill level, infer seniority, score ATS compatibility, or predict interviews. Never add a tool, language, certification, date, employer, or achievement you cannot verify.
