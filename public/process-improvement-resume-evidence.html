Review method

Make the comparison reproducible

  1. Define the original process and an accurate baseline.
  2. Record the change you personally proposed or implemented.
  3. Use the same metric definition and a valid comparison period.
  4. Separate your contribution from team, tooling, volume, and market effects.

Boundary

No causal or performance verification

The map does not verify analytics, prove causation, assess performance, score ATS compatibility, or predict hiring. Never invent baselines, dates, metrics, savings, tools, ownership, approvals, or outcomes.

Protect operational data

Do not paste customer data, private financials, employee records, credentials, internal dashboards, contracts, or confidential process documentation.
