One role at a time

Separate facts from selection

  1. Record the employer, actual title, start and end dates, work type, and evidence source.
  2. Map supported tasks, skills, and outcomes to an explicit target-role requirement.
  3. Mark each role included, compressed, or omitted for review; preserve the factual master history separately.
  4. Flag date gaps, overlaps, title ambiguity, and disclosure questions for a qualified human reviewer.

Compression

Shorten detail without joining unrelated roles

A compressed role can retain accurate employer, title, and dates with fewer evidence-backed bullets. Never merge employers, extend dates, rename titles, or turn gaps into employment. Do not convert gig, volunteer, coursework, or informal help into a different work category.

Selection boundary

No universal disclosure decision

JobProofAI does not decide what an employer requires you to disclose, whether omitting a role is appropriate, or how a background check will interpret a record. Requirements vary by application, jurisdiction, regulated role, employer, and verification process. Review the actual instructions and ask a qualified adviser when the consequence matters.

No hiring prediction

Evidence organization only

The guide does not score ATS compatibility, infer recruiter preference, verify employment, contact employers, run a background check, submit an application, or predict interviews or hiring. Missing evidence remains missing.

Keep the working map private

Remove addresses, employee IDs, manager contacts, confidential metrics, customer data, credentials, and unrelated personal information. Use only records you are permitted to review.
