Section 1

Requirement-to-evidence workbook

For each requirement, preserve the employer's exact wording, point to an existing resume line or record, label the support as direct, partial, or missing, and write a question that can only be answered from real experience.

Section 2

Claim-verification table

  • Claim being considered.
  • Source record and date checked.
  • Your actual role versus team contribution.
  • Metric definition, period, and attribution.
  • Remove or revise when proof is absent.

Section 3

Final human-review checklist

  1. Every employer, role, date, credential, tool, and responsibility is accurate.
  2. Every number has a source and defensible definition.
  3. Team outcomes do not become unsupported personal claims.
  4. Sensitive employer, client, complaint, or investigation details are removed.
  5. The final resume is reviewed by the applicant before use.
